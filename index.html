Support

Banners: one video, on every screen.

Questions, a problem with a purchase, or want to report a video? Email us at bannerssuport@gmail.com. We reply within 2 business days, and handle reported videos within 24 hours.

  • Report a video: tap ⋯ on the live video, then Report.
  • Block a poster: tap ⋯, then Block poster.
  • Refunds: if your video is rejected before it airs, your credits come back automatically. For App Store charges, see reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete my account & data.
