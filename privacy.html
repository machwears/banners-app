Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

What we collect

  • An account ID. It's created automatically when you open the app. We don't ask for your name or phone number.
  • Your email address, only if you choose Sign in with Apple. It may be a private relay address from Apple. We use it only to identify your account.
  • Videos you upload. They are shown publicly to everyone using the app if approved.
  • Bids, credit purchases and balance. This includes App Store transaction IDs. We never see your card details.
  • Reports and blocks you make.
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications. We use it only to tell you about your own videos, such as when yours is next or wasn't approved.
  • Waitlist email. If you sign up on our website, we store your email only to tell you when Banners launches. Email us to be removed.

How we use it

We use it only to run the app: show videos, run the auction, credit purchases, and moderate content. We don't sell data, show third-party ads, or track you across apps.

Service providers

  • Supabase (database, storage and hosting)
  • Apple (In-App Purchase, Sign in with Apple, push notifications)

Retention and deletion

Go to Settings → "Delete my account & data". This permanently removes your videos, bids, credits and account, and revokes Sign in with Apple access. Remaining credits are forfeited.

Children

Banners is not directed at children under 13.
